What you are viewing: results from seven ngspice runs of an averaged behavioral model, with a simplified 2.5 Ah battery. This is not the manufacturer’s LT3652 model and does not prove that the physical PCB works. The LEDs below follow simulated branch currents.

The model’s estimated state of charge is not a real battery measurement. Startup transients and switching ripple are outside this model’s scope.
How do I re-run or modify the actual simulation?

Use ngspice, the simulator used to produce these results. The browser plays the included results; it does not execute SPICE.

Open a terminal in the spice folder and enter ngspice 01_normal_charge.cir. At its prompt enter run, then plot v(bat) or plot i(vsense). LED currents are i(vled_green) and i(vled_red).

With Python 3 installed, python run_simulations.py --ngspice PATH_TO_NGSPICE re-runs all seven test cases and rebuilds this self-contained page. Read README.md for assumptions, parameters, and limitations.
