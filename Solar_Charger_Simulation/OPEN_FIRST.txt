SOLAR CHARGER SIMULATION DEMONSTRATION

1. Extract the complete ZIP.
2. Open START_HERE.html in Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Firefox.
3. Choose a test case and press Play results. Use the time slider or event buttons.

No simulator installation is needed for viewing.
To modify and re-run the actual .cir circuit files, install ngspice; see README.md.
Simulation_Report.pdf contains presentation-ready plots.

This is an educational averaged behavioral model, NOT the manufacturer LT3652 model.
Its seven completed SPICE runs passed 40 numerical checks. It does not prove the physical PCB works.
